About Me

Hello there! myself Avaneesh, a boy who loves nature and natural places.I like to embrace the beauty of nature. Along with it, i also like fishes (both ornamental and native) and it has been six years of keeping fishes. It all started in the year 2020 when i had very little knowledge about fishes. I got inspiration from various tanks i used to see and finally urged my father to buy an aquarium. i still remember my first aquarium was about 2.5ft in length and had a height of 2 ft. it has pink stones at the bottom and was stacked with fishes (goldfish,angelfish,gourami,carp and pleco). As there was lockdown due to Covid-19 i spent time doing research about fish keeping and it helped me accquire knowledge about building aquarium myself and giving fishes longetivity. And in 2022 i bought my second aquarium which i made planted by adding Anubias, Hygrophilia, Rotala and Ludwigia. After that i also brought a bow(i made it planted too!) about 30L to house a red Betta, i tamed it to take feeding from my fingers, it gave much happy. I also added cherry shrimp in my second tank and to my surprise they bred!

I also started going to fishing with my friend, our fishing spots include kharun river checkdam and Arpa river. The checkdam on Kharun is a wonderful spot to catch ghost shrimp and black diamond shrimp, you just have to net the flow and you already have so many shrimp. The arpa river is very clean and tidy bedore entering the city so there is alot of chances of catching red lily Gourami and badis. Shrimps are also found here but better to go to Kharun if you want more colourful ones.
